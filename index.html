02 / THE MECHANISM

A simple
message rule.

Each member sends a fraction of their current signal along each tie. A noisy-OR combines incoming messages while preserving an individual baseline.

pᵢ (t+1)=1 − (1 − pᵢ⁰) ∏j ∈ N(i) (1 − β · wⱼᵢ · pⱼ(t))

pᵢ⁰synthetic baseline risk for member i

βthe message-strength control

wⱼᵢtie strength from member j to i

pⱼ(t)member j’s signal in the current round

This compact rule is for explanation, not inference. Real churn models need validated data, leakage controls, calibration, time-aware evaluation, governance and a clear intervention policy.

03 / THE PROFESSIONAL STORY

Built from a real question.
Shown with a new example.

At Nextel, I developed a proprietary graph-based churn model called Favorites. It drew on network influence, message-passing ideas and probabilistic graphical models to examine how churn by an influential member could change risk across their close network. I explored graph tooling including NetworkX and graph databases.
